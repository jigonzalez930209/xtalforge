# End-to-end audit and visual validation

The `1.1.0` API is verified against independent reference implementations and
against deterministic 3D frames. The battery lives in `tests/e2e/` and runs
through Playwright against a built VitePress site; it is also the regression
gate for the [3D playground](/playground).

## Reference implementations

| Domain | Reference | What it provides |
|---|---|---|
| CIF parsing, symmetry expansion, cell and density | **gemmi** 0.7.5 | Expanded P1 sites, cell parameters and the atom list used for the position comparison |
| Space group and primitive cell | **spglib** 2.8.0 | International number and primitive volume |
| Lattice matrix, periodic neighbours, nanotubes | **ASE** 3.29.0 | Cartesian cell, shortest periodic distance and the `(5, 5)` tube geometry |
| Bragg angles | **NumPy** | Reciprocal metric, `d` spacing and Cu Kα `2θ` positions |
| Visual frames | Committed PNG goldens | Pixel comparison of the deterministic canvas |

The reference JSON is generated by `tests/e2e/reference/generate.py` and is
committed; the browser never reads it.

## What the battery exercises

The suite has 68 checks (49 from the first audit, plus the porosity, MOF and
refinement-panel checks against Python, the Wyckoff letter
checks against spglib for the eight samples, the PXRD intensity
checks for Si, NaCl, TiO2 and Fe and the guided tour). For every sample in `Si`, `NaCl`, `diamond`,
`graphene`, `TiO2`, `MFI`, `MOF-5` and `Fe` it verifies:

- formula, cell volume and density against gemmi;
- P1 atom positions against gemmi modulo lattice translations (maximum error
  is reported in Ångström and required to be below 5 mÅ);
- detected space group against spglib;
- the 12 cell-edge segments against the ASE lattice;
- the projected atom centres against an independent reimplementation of the
  camera used by the renderer;
- the shortest perceived bond against the ASE periodic neighbour list
  (metal-metal contacts are intentionally absent from the bond graph).

It also covers:

- `2 x 2 x 2` supercells (volume, atom count and scene geometry);
- `(1 1 1)` slabs with two layers and 10 Å of vacuum (surface area, thickness,
  cell height, atom count and position range);
- primitive cell, standardized cell, P1 expansion, asymmetric unit and Niggli
  reduction;
- graphene, `(5, 5)` nanotubes, C60, C20 and a `pcu` MOF;
- Cu Kα PXRD peak positions for Si, NaCl, TiO2, Fe and graphene;
- relative Cu Kα PXRD intensities for Si, NaCl, TiO2 and Fe against
  `|F|^2 m LP` with gemmi's IT92 form factors (within 1 % of the strongest
  line);
- the seventeen-step playground tour (zone counts, the tetrahedral angle, the
  NaCl supercell, the twisted-bilayer cell and every builder step);
- Brillouin-zone vertex, face and edge counts for fcc silicon and rock salt,
  bcc iron, the graphene hexagonal prism and the orthorhombic MFI cell;
- the playground tabs, Brillouin view, atom selection distances/angles/
  torsions, nanoribbon/polymer/Schwarz P builders, powder plot, UFF energy,
  POSCAR export and fullscreen;
- deterministic screenshots for eight samples, six operations and the
  space-fill style;
- the cell-volume, CIF-inspector and document-editor demos.

## Findings fixed by the audit

The battery exposed four real defects, all fixed in the same cycle:

1. **Supercells dropped the symmetry expansion.** `Structure::supercell`
   copied the asymmetric sites, so `xtalforge supercell Si.cif 2 2 2` wrote
   8 atoms instead of 64 (and the WASM playground showed the same cell). The
   core now expands to P1 first through `Structure::as_p1`; the CLI atom-count
   estimate uses the P1 count and the CLI tests expect the full cell.
2. **`is_p1` trusted only the Hall symbol.** A CIF with an explicit
   `_symmetry_equiv_pos_as_xyz` loop but no space-group name was labelled
   `P 1`, so formula, density and charge ignored the operations. `is_p1` now
   requires every operation to be the identity as well.
3. **The CIF-inspector demo shipped an incomplete silicon CIF.** Its eight
   point operations had no centring translations, so the reader correctly
   produced one atom (`Si`, 0.29 g/cm³) while the page claimed silicon. The
   demo now uses the Hall symbol `F 4d 2 3 -1d` and reports `Si8` and
   2.329 g/cm³.
4. **The WASM surface could not feed a viewer.** It only returned scene
   counts, summaries had no P1 atom count, and transforms/builders returned no
   structure text. The audit added `scene_data`, `n_atoms` and text-returning
   variants, plus Python `scene_data` and the CLI `mesh --scene` geometry
   dump, so the browser draws the same Rust scene instead of reimplementing
   geometry.
5. **The Brillouin zone of a conventional centred cell was a cube.** The zone
   was built from `structure.lattice()`, so silicon (fcc), rock salt (fcc) and
   bcc iron all returned the reciprocal cell of the conventional cube instead
   of the primitive zone. `brillouin_zone_for_structure` now detects the
   primitive cell first, and the clipping uses an initial cube large enough
   for anisotropic cells (graphene returned a box instead of its hexagonal
   prism). The zone counts are now checked for fcc, bcc, simple cubic,
   orthorhombic and hexagonal lattices.

## Functional audit of 2026-11-11

A second pass ran every CLI subcommand on the samples and compared the
results with independent calculations (gemmi IT92 structure factors, spglib,
ASE neighbour lists and geometry checks). It found and fixed:

6. **PXRD relative intensities.** The form factor was the Z-based Gaussian
   for every element; rutile `(1 1 1)` came out at 28 % of `(1 1 0)` instead of
   19 % and silicon `(2 2 0)` at 77 % instead of 67 %. The International
   Tables (1992) coefficients are now embedded and the Si, NaCl, Fe and rutile
   lines agree with the independent `|F|^2 m LP` to 0.1 %.
7. **Twisted bilayers ignored the angle.** `build twist 2 1 21.79` returned an
   untwisted AA bilayer (any angle was rounded to 0 or 60 degrees). The
   builder now constructs the commensurate moire cell; every carbon keeps three
   1.42 Angstrom neighbours in its layer.
8. **Polymers overlapped themselves.** A six-unit polyethylene chain was
   packed into a 6.2 Angstrom cube, giving C-C contacts of 0.67 Angstrom. The
   chain is now periodic along `a`, joined head to tail.
9. **SMILES rings collapsed.** The embedder had no angle term; benzene came
   out non-planar with 1.17 Angstrom 1-3 contacts. Hybridization-based angle
   targets fix rings, chains and alkynes.
10. **Disordered carbon contacts.** `build carbon` produced carbons 0.15
    Angstrom apart and terminations colliding across narrow gaps. The dual
    graph is relaxed to 1.42 Angstrom/120 degrees, terminations follow the
    free in-plane valence, overlapping terminations are dropped and every bond
    that crosses the cell boundary carries its lattice image (71 bonds of the
    default build were drawn across the whole cell).
11. **Playground defaults.** The twisted-bilayer form called the builder with
    `m = 0`, which is rejected.
12. **Vibrations of crystals were nonsense.** `ff vibrations Si.cif` reported
    modes of -13 847 and +16 528 cm^-1. The P1 expansion turned a coordinate
    of -1e-32 (a Cartesian round trip of 0) into exactly 1.0, which
    duplicated an atom in the UFF view (+1028 eV). Coordinates now stay in
    `[0, 1)` (`P1Cell::expand`, `Lattice::wrap`) and silicon has three zero
    acoustic modes and triply degenerate optical modes (347-749 cm^-1 with
    UFF).
13. **Le Bail and Pawley ignored the background.** With a 50-count baseline
    Le Bail reached R = 0.69 even from the true cell, because the partition
    inflated the peaks to absorb the baseline. Both now subtract a SNIP
    envelope (about ten peak widths) and Le Bail uses the standard
    profile-weighted partition; R drops to 0.27 on the same data.
14. **MOF node orientation.** The greedy rule left the in-plane angle of
    planar nodes arbitrary, so `srs` linkers were stretched 0.55-1.26 times.
    A Kabsch fit over every connection-to-edge assignment now orients the
    nodes.
15. **ZTC and Schwarz P carbons were not sp2.** They are now the relaxed dual
    of the remeshed surface (see below); the sampled grid also gained the
    closing plane that the marching tetrahedra need for a closed surface.
16. **Wyckoff letters used moyo's origin.** Silicon at `0, 0, 0` in origin
    choice 1 was reported as `8b`. When the input basis is the conventional one
    (or a signed axis permutation in a cubic group) the letter is now matched
    on the input coordinates; all eight samples agree atom by atom with spglib.
17. **EEQ was QEq.** `eeq_charges` reused the QEq table and returned the QEq
    charges. It is now EEQ2019 and matches the sci-form/dftd4 reference for
    water and methanol to 2e-3.
18. **Rietveld cell refinement was local.** From a rutile cell 1 % off it
    stopped at Rwp 0.47 with `c = 2.932` (true 2.989). A Pawley cell stage
    (`pawley_cell`) now runs first; the same start recovers `a = 4.6399` and
    `c = 2.9890` (Rwp 0.12).
19. **Incompatible MOF blocks were built.** Two metal-free carboxylate blocks
    (`benzene_tri` with `benzene`) placed oxygens 0.6 Angstrom apart; the
    builder now refuses them with an `invalid_input` error.
20. **ZTC/Schwarz P below their minimum cell.** Below 12 Angstrom (gyroid) or
    8 Angstrom (Schwarz P) fewer than 90 % of the carbons are
    three-coordinate; the builders now refuse such cells, and still reject
    fewer than two samples (the new WASM suite run caught that regression).
21. **Surface differences.** A field-by-field comparison of CLI, Python and
    WASM on the eight samples (`scripts/check-surfaces.sh`) found: `eeq` was
    missing from Python and WASM; the CLI `info` JSON had no `n_atoms`; twelve
    WASM errors were thrown as bare strings without `Error.code`; and the PXRD
    reflections with equal two-theta came out in a different order in WASM
    (last-bit differences in the sort key). All four are fixed and the 91
    compared fields now agree.
22. **Periodic EEQ was not converged.** The real-space Coulomb cutoff gave
    MOF-5 Zn at +4.4. EEQ now uses an Ewald sum for the Gaussian charges; a
    rutile 2 x 1 x 1 supercell repeats the unit-cell charges to 1e-4 and
    MOF-5 ranges from -0.58 to +0.79.
23. **The Rietveld phase fit ignored the background.** `fit_phase` documents
    a scale and background fit but kept the background at its start value
    (0), so the automatic run reported Rwp 1.05 on the reference rutile
    pattern. Scale and background are now a weighted linear least-squares
    pair; the same fit gives Rwp 0.33 (the rest is the fixed width).
24. **Le Bail existed only in the CLI.** Python `powder_lebail` and WASM
    `powder_lebail_info` were added, with a parity-matrix row; WASM gained
    `powder_pawley_width_info` because its Pawley could not refine the size
    and strain widths that Python can (Rwp 0.31 to 0.21 on rutile).
25. **CLI and bindings disagreed on Rietveld.** The CLI forced 30
    iterations where Python and WASM use the library default; it now uses the
    default and the three surfaces agree.
26. **The CLI panicked on a closed pipe.** `xtalforge ... | head` printed a
    panic and a backtrace hint; it now exits quietly with status 141.
27. **The refinement panel showed the indexed cell as the Pawley cell.** The
    automatic result exposed only the quick cubic-index edge as `cell`, and
    the panel printed the leading row's indexing candidate (a = 4.5902
    Angstrom for rutile) while the Pawley fit had refined it. CLI, Python and
    WASM now return `best_cell` from the Pawley stage, falling back to the
    leading indexed cell when Pawley is skipped; rutile reports a = 4.5993
    Angstrom, and the panel and CLI print it.
28. **Playground state after a transform.** Running a builder or transform
    left the sample selector on a stale sample id; it now shows "Built or
    transformed". The nanotube tour step looks from +x so the tube lies
    across the view instead of end-on.

Verified as correct in the same pass: formula, volume and density of the eight
samples; space groups; bond counts, ring census and Si-Si distances; C60,
C20, graphene, ribbon and `(5, 5)` nanotube bond lengths; QEq/EQeq charge
neutrality; supercells, slabs and the Brillouin zones; analytic UFF forces
and the Gamma-point Hessian against central differences of the energy
(`crates/xtalforge-ff/tests/uff_gradient.rs`); the general indexing of a
synthetic rutile pattern (a = 4.6403, c = 2.9870 against 4.6400 and 2.9890);
Pawley cell refinement from a 1 % expanded start (a = 4.6399); Rietveld
from a near cell (a = 4.6399, c = 2.9890, Rwp 0.12) and the automatic
powder run; MOF assembly of pcu/sql/Cr3O frameworks (C-C 1.35-1.48
Angstrom).

## Known gaps

- Polymers carry the heavy-atom backbone only; hydrogens are added with
  `hydrogens` afterwards.

- The battery covers the structure, symmetry, builder, view and powder-position
  surfaces. `scripts/check-surfaces.sh` adds UFF energies, QEq/EQeq/EEQ
  charges, MFI porosity, the pcu/Zn4O/benzene MOF and Le Bail, Pawley,
  Rietveld and the automatic run on the reference rutile pattern, on the CLI,
  Python and WASM surfaces (97 fields). The browser runner checks the
  porosity panel, the MOF builder and the refinement panel (all four methods)
  against the Python results.
- Wyckoff multiplicities are compared per atom with the spglib orbit sizes,
  divided by the centring factor when a sample is stored in its primitive
  cell (MOF-5); the earlier MOF-5 exclusion is gone.
- Screenshot goldens are renderer- and platform-specific. They catch changes
  in the scene, the camera and the layout, not differences between browser
  GPUs; the numeric projection check is the platform-independent evidence for
  the 3D geometry.
- The WASM bundle measures 3940 KiB against a 3968 KiB budget. The budget
  was 3904 KiB after the scene and text exports and was raised by this audit
  for the fixes listed above; any further surface helper needs a deliberate
  size review.
- The CLI, Python and WASM surfaces have their own integration suites; the
  Playwright battery targets the WASM surface because it is the one a browser
  user sees. `scripts/check-parity.sh` keeps the four surfaces aligned.

## Run it

```bash
scripts/e2e.sh                  # rebuild WASM + docs and run all checks
scripts/e2e.sh --skip-build     # reuse the current dist
scripts/e2e.sh --update-goldens # review and accept new screenshots
```

Captured PNGs and the assertion output are written to
`tests/e2e/test-results/`, which is ignored by git. The committed goldens live
in `tests/e2e/golden/`.
