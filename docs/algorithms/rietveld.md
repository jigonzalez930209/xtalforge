# Rietveld refinement

## Stage order

![Rietveld stages](/diagrams/rietveld-stages.svg)


`refine_phase` follows the McCusker order. Each stage changes only its own
parameters and leaves the rest at the value the previous stage reached:

| Stage | Parameters | Method |
|---|---|---|
| `background_scale` | scale and the background polynomial | analytic weighted least squares |
| `zero_cell` | zero shift and the free cell numbers | Nelder-Mead simplex |
| `width` | multiplier on the PXRD FWHM | Nelder-Mead simplex |
| `structure` | Wyckoff coordinates, isotropic ADPs, occupancies, March-Dollase texture | Nelder-Mead simplex |

The scale and a constant, linear or quadratic background are linear, so they
are solved directly at every model evaluation. The other parameters move the
reflection positions or the relative intensities and are refined by a bounded
direct search.

## Why a direct search

The zero shift, the cell and the width are strongly correlated: a small cell
change can be absorbed by a width change, and a zero shift can be absorbed by
the cell. At a displaced start the Gauss-Newton direction can point uphill,
while a simplex still finds the valley. Every simplex vertex is projected onto
the parameter bounds and the scale/background are refitted before the vertex is
scored, so the search compares complete models.

## Wyckoff-allowed coordinates

A site may only move along the directions its stabilizer leaves invariant. For
every symmetry operation `R` that maps the site onto itself modulo a lattice
translation, a displacement `u` keeps the site on the same Wyckoff position when
`R u = u`. Intersecting that condition gives:

- three directions on a general position,
- one on a mirror or a rotation axis,
- none at an inversion centre.

`free_directions(operations, frac)` returns an orthonormal deterministic basis
of that subspace. The refinement creates one parameter per basis vector, so a
rutile oxygen at `4f (x, x, 0)` moves only along `[1, 1, 0] / sqrt(2)` and a
site at the origin does not move at all.

## Cell family ties

Only the numbers the metric family leaves free are refined, and the ties are
restored after every move: a cubic cell keeps `a = b = c`, a tetragonal or
hexagonal cell keeps `a = b`, and the angles of the higher-symmetry families
stay at their base values. Without the ties, a refined `a` would no longer
equal the untouched `b` and symmetry-equivalent reflections would split apart.

## Texture

The March-Dollase factor for a reflection with unit normal `n` and a
preferred-orientation axis `a` is

```text
r^2 cos^2(theta) + sin^2(theta) / r,  raised to -3/2,
```

with `cos(theta) = n . a`. `r = 1` is random orientation. In this port the axis
is fixed by `RefineOptions::texture_axis` and only `r` is refined.

## Standard uncertainties

At the final values the Jacobian is built by central differences of the
**complete** model, so the analytic scale/background refit is already projected
into every column. The covariance is `(J^T W J)^-1` scaled by the reduced
chi-squared, and the diagonal gives the parameter esds. The scale and the
background coefficients are fitted analytically and are reported without esds.

Occupancies and the scale are degenerate: multiplying every occupancy by `c`
and dividing the scale by `c` leaves the pattern unchanged. Only the occupancy
ratios are observable, and the reported absolute values follow the fitted
scale. This is why the CLI enables occupancy refinement only with
`--occupancies`.

## Validation

`crates/xtalforge-analysis/tests/rietveld_golden.rs` reads
`tests/golden/phase-7/rietveld/rutile.xy`, generated by the Crystal-Builder
`_rutile_xy` fixture (rutile, `P4_2/mnm`, `a = 4.5940`, `c = 2.9590`, oxygen at
`x = 0.29`, Cu K alpha 1 + K alpha 2, 20-80 degrees, Poisson noise with seed 0).
The structure starts at `x = 0.26`; the McCusker stages must return it to the
generating position:

- `x` within `3e-3` of `0.29`, still on `(x, x, 0)`,
- `a` and `c` within `2e-3` Angstrom, with `a = b` exactly,
- zero shift within `0.02` degree,
- `Rwp < 0.25`, every requested parameter with a positive esd,
- stages exactly `background_scale -> zero_cell -> width -> structure`.

The synthetic tests in `tests/rietveld_stages.rs` add exact recoveries of a
known zero shift, cell, width multiplier, March-Dollase ratio, ADP multiplier,
occupancy ratio and the Wyckoff direction counts.

## Checked on a second structure

A noisy, backgrounded NaCl pattern (Poisson noise, 150 counts of background,
single wavelength) refined from a starting cell of 5.64 Angstrom returns
`a = 5.620`, `width_scale = 0.997` and a zero shift of 0.000 with Rwp 0.094 when
the model uses one wavelength (`doublet = false`, CLI `--no-doublet`). With the
doublet model on single-wavelength data the isotropic ADP multiplier runs to
its bound to compensate (Rwp 0.26, `a = 5.615`), and on a noise-free pattern
with zero background the `1 / y` weights drive Rwp towards 0.7: match the
radiation and give the data a background or an uncertainty column.

## Documented deviations

- The profile is the PXRD engine area-normalised Gaussian for every reflection;
  `RietveldOptions::kind` is accepted but does not change this curve. The
  reference uses a pseudo-Voigt with a Caglioti width.
- With `doublet = true` the copper K alpha 1/K alpha 2 pair is placed from the
  K alpha 1 position with the 0.5 intensity ratio. Other radiations use one
  line. This is why the golden `Rwp` is `0.15`, not the `~0.05` of the rietx
  reference, which uses the full instrument profile.
- The isotropic ADP is one global multiplier on every site's `U_iso`, not one
  free `U_ij` per site.
- The energy term of `combined_objective` needs a force-field calculator that
  this signature does not have, so it stays at zero during the fit.
